# Tokyo Night syntax colors in Anden

`extensions/theme-defaults/themes/anden-dark.json` and `extensions/theme-defaults/themes/anden-light.json` use the TextMate rules, semantic token rules, and ANSI colors of [Tokyo Night](https://github.com/tokyo-night/tokyo-night-vscode-theme/tree/7c0f11eaef322f293621ca7befe462214b7ea468), from `themes/tokyo-night-color-theme.json` and `themes/tokyo-night-light-color-theme.json`, respectively. Anden supplies the workbench and diff colors. The upstream MIT license is included in [tokyo-night-MIT.txt](tokyo-night-MIT.txt).
